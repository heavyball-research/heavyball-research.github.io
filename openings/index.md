We are recruiting **PhD students** and **research interns** to work on efficient AI models, training and inference algorithms, and ML systems.

## PhD Students

Join us through the [NYU Shanghai Computer Science PhD program](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/page/computer-science-phd-program)! Students earn an NYU degree, complete coursework in New York, and conduct their doctoral research at NYU Shanghai.

The NYU Shanghai Doctoral Fellowship covers tuition and fees and provides an annual stipend, with additional support for health insurance, housing while in New York, and research travel. See the [program page](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/page/computer-science-phd-program) for eligibility, application requirements, and funding details.

Students interested in remote advising or co-advising with faculty in New York are welcome to discuss these options with [Yucheng](https://www.yucheng-lu.me/).

## Research Interns

Research internship openings become available throughout the year. If you are interested in joining us, please email [Yucheng](https://www.yucheng-lu.me/) with your current CV and a short description of your research interests.
